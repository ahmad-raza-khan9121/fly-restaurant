Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

What This Page Covers

Fly Restaurant's website is a simple front-end site. This policy explains, plainly, what data the site stores and where it goes — so there are no surprises.

Data Stored On Your Own Device

Your Cart, Wishlist, and Account (login) details are saved using your browser's local storage (localStorage) — directly on your own device. This data is not sent to or stored on any server owned by Fly Restaurant. Clearing your browser data or using a different browser/device will remove it.

Account / Login

The "Account" login on this site is a simple front-end demo — it checks that you've entered a valid-looking email and password, and remembers your name locally so the site can greet you. It is not a secure authentication system and should not be used with a real password you use elsewhere.

Contact & Reservation Form

Submitting the contact/reservation form opens your device's email app with your message pre-filled, addressed to the restaurant's email. Fly Restaurant only receives what you choose to send from your own email app — nothing is transmitted automatically in the background.

Newsletter Signup

The newsletter box currently stores the email you enter locally in your browser, for demonstration purposes. It is not yet connected to a real mailing list service.

Orders via WhatsApp

Placing an order opens WhatsApp with your order details pre-filled. That message is sent through WhatsApp directly to the restaurant's number, subject to WhatsApp's own privacy policy.

Third-Party Resources

This site loads fonts from Google Fonts and icons from the Font Awesome CDN (via cloudflare), and embeds a Google Map on the Contact page. These third parties may process standard technical data (like your IP address) as part of delivering those resources, under their own respective privacy policies.
